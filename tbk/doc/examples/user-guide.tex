\tbkChapter{Installation}

\tbkSection{Before You Begin}

\tbkTip{Take a photo of your existing thermostat wiring before disconnecting any wires.}

\tbkSection{Installation Steps}

\begin{tbkSteps}
  \item Turn off power to your HVAC system at the circuit breaker.
  \item Remove your old thermostat from the wall.
  \item Label each wire using the provided wire labels.
  \item Disconnect wires from the old thermostat.
  \item Mount the new wall plate using the provided screws.
  \item Connect wires to the corresponding terminals on the wall plate.
  \item Attach the thermostat to the wall plate.
  \item Turn power back on at the circuit breaker.
  \item Follow the on-screen setup wizard.
\end{tbkSteps}

\tbkSection{Temperature Modes}

\tbkSubsection{Heating Mode}

In heating mode \tbkIcon{arrow-up}, the thermostat activates your heating system when the temperature falls below the target.

\tbkSubsection{Cooling Mode}

In cooling mode \tbkIcon{arrow-down}, the thermostat activates your cooling system when the temperature rises above the target.

\tbkSubsection{Auto Mode}

In auto mode, the thermostat automatically switches between heating and cooling to maintain comfort.

\tbkChapter{WiFi Setup}

\begin{tbkSteps}
  \item Press \tbkIcon{settings} to open settings.
  \item Select ``WiFi Setup''.
  \item Choose your network from the list.
  \item Enter your WiFi password using the on-screen keyboard.
  \item Press ``Connect''.
\end{tbkSteps}

\tbkSuccess{Once connected, you can control your thermostat from anywhere using the SmartHome app!}

\tbkChapter{Troubleshooting}

\tbkSection{Common Issues}

\tbkSubsection{Thermostat Not Responding}

\begin{tbkList}
  \item Check that power is connected
  \item Verify circuit breaker is on
  \item Try removing and reattaching the thermostat from the wall plate
\end{tbkList}

\tbkSubsection{WiFi Connection Problems}

\begin{tbkList}
  \item Ensure your router is working
  \item Check that you're connecting to a 2.4GHz network (5GHz not supported)
  \item Move closer to your router during setup
  \item Restart your router
\end{tbkList}
