Follow these steps to set up your device:

\begin{tbkSteps}[prefix=Step]
  \item Unpack the device and remove all protective materials.
  \item Position the table on a flat, stable surface.
  \item Connect the power cable to a grounded outlet.
  \item Connect peripherals (keyboard, mouse, network cable).
  \item Press the power button to turn on the device.
  \item Follow the on-screen setup wizard.
